# Market research: declarative desktop-action executors (2025–2026)

Newer entrants worth knowing: [enigo](https://github.com/enigo-rs/enigo) (Rust cross-platform input crate, MIT, active, 1.8k★ — X11 solid, **Wayland/libei experimental with known bugs and US-layout-only caveats**; used/forked by RustDesk), [espanso](https://espanso.org/docs/install/linux/) (Rust, proves cross-platform injection incl. Wayland EVDEV is shippable — at the cost of `CAP_DAC_OVERRIDE` and "experimental" Wayland status), [Botwork](https://github.com/dhilipsiva/botwork) (Rust single-binary Robot-Framework alternative — stalled at 0.2, ~2023, validates the itch but not the market).

## 2. The gap

**Direct answer: no maintained, cross-platform, single-binary CLI that executes desktop input from declarative files with built-in verification exists as of mid-2026.** Everything is missing at least two legs:

- **[terminator (mediar-ai)](https://github.com/mediar-ai/terminator)** — the closest thing in spirit: Rust core, MIT, CLI + YAML workflows, "deterministic workflows, AI only for recovery, >95% success," $2.8M raised, active (1.6k★). But **Windows-only** and accessibility-tree-based, not coordinate-based.
- **[agent-desktop](https://github.com/lahfir/agent-desktop)** — Rust single binary, structured JSON, exit codes, deterministic element refs. **macOS-only** (Win/Linux "planned"), a11y-tree-based, agent-oriented rather than declarative-file-oriented.
- **[Maestro](https://maestro.dev/)** — proves the declarative-YAML-flow UX is loved (`tapOn`, `assertVisible`, auto-wait/retry) — but mobile/web only.
- **[openQA/os-autoinst](https://open.qa/docs/)** — proves the *exact* pixelcoords/pixelactions data model at industrial scale: SUSE/openSUSE QA runs on "needles" = [PNG screenshot + JSON of labeled areas with match thresholds and click points](https://github.com/os-autoinst/os-autoinst-needles-openQA), fuzzy template matching, assertion-driven. But it's a Perl monolith welded to QEMU/VNC for OS-image testing, not a desktop CLI.

What people actually cobble: platform-conditional shell (`xdotool` on X11, `ydotool`/`dotool` + udev rules on Wayland, `cliclick`/AppleScript on macOS, AHK/PowerShell on Windows) glued with sleeps and prayer. Evidence of the seam: [SeleniumBase users asking for Wayland fallbacks to PyAutoGUI actions](https://github.com/seleniumbase/SeleniumBase/issues/4010), [touchegg debating ydotool vs dotool](https://github.com/JoseExposito/touchegg/issues/656), [AI tools shipping "X11 only" caveats](https://github.com/DonTizi/ReMind/issues/6), and a cottage industry of "PyAutoGUI alternatives" listicles ([TestDriver's](https://testdriver.ai/articles/top-12-alternatives-to-pyautogui-for-windows-macos-linux-testing) recommend... browser tools and Appium — i.e., nothing actually fills it). On Wayland, the real 2025–2026 development is plumbing-level: [libei + the XDG RemoteDesktop/InputCapture portals](http://who-t.blogspot.com/2026/07/libei-integrations-in-xdg-remotedesktop.html), with XTEST→libei translation in XWayland — portal-mediated, permission-prompted injection is becoming the sanctioned path, and almost no end-user tool has wrapped it well yet.

## 3. Computer-use agents as a market force

How they execute today:
- **Anthropic computer use**: the [reference quickstart](https://github.com/anthropics/anthropic-quickstarts/blob/main/computer-use-demo/Dockerfile) is an Ubuntu/Xvfb Docker container where the ComputerTool [shells out to xdotool for clicks and scrot for screenshots](https://simonwillison.net/2024/Oct/22/computer-use/); the model emits pixel coordinates from screenshots.
- **OpenAI Operator → ChatGPT agent**: [CUA model emits click/type/scroll actions against screenshots; "your harness executes those actions"](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/tools-computer-use) — OpenAI runs its own [cloud virtual browser/computer](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-agent/); the API pushes execution onto you.
- **Open-source/research**: [OSWorld standardized on raw `pyautogui` as the action space](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.07972), so UI-TARS, Agent S2 and most academic agents literally emit pyautogui snippets — inheriting all its platform holes. Sandbox vendors ([E2B desktop](https://e2b.dev/docs/use-cases/computer-use), [trycua/cua](https://github.com/trycua/cua) — YC-backed, MIT, cross-OS sandboxes + MCP server, [Bytebot](https://github.com/topics/cua)) each ship their own injector inside a VM/container.
- **MCP desktop-control servers are proliferating and thin**: [computer-control-mcp (PyAutoGUI+OCR)](https://github.com/AB498/computer-control-mcp), [MCPControl (Windows)](https://github.com/claude-did-this/MCPControl), [mcp-desktop-automation (robotjs, 10MB screenshot limits)](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/@tanob/mcp-desktop-automation), [a Rust computer-use MCP for Win/mac](https://github.com/zavora-ai/computer-use-mcp), [Windows-MCP](https://mcp.directory/blog/windows-mcp-guide). Mostly wrappers over the same broken substrates — none offer verified, replayable execution.

Demand signal for a deterministic executor is real and articulated: terminator's whole pitch is *"pre-train workflows as deterministic code, call AI only when recovery is needed"*; [Microsoft Copilot Studio computer use](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/blog/copilot-studio/computer-using-agents-now-deliver-more-secure-ui-automation-at-scale/) sells auditability; research keeps documenting that [CUAs misclick and misground on professional UIs](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.17849v1), spawning [pre-execution click-verification guardrails](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.14707) and ["deterministic replay" as a missing primitive](https://www.sakurasky.com/blog/missing-primitives-for-trustworthy-ai-part-8/). An agent that *plans once* and then replays a pixelcoords-verified flow — with point-asserts gating each step and exit codes on drift — is exactly the "LLM for flexibility, deterministic execution for correctness" architecture practitioners are converging on.

## 4. Adjacent validation (GUI test automation)

- **[WinAppDriver is dead](https://testguild.com/automation-tools-desktop/)** — no release since Nov 2020, 1,100+ open issues, closed-source server, obsolete JSON Wire protocol; [Appium's Windows driver is hostage to it](https://github.com/appium/appium-windows-driver), with [FlaUI (MIT, v5.0.0 Feb 2025)](https://github.com/FlaUI/FlaUI.WebDriver) and NovaWindows as the .NET escape hatches. Windows desktop test automation is in a vacuum Microsoft declined to fill.
- **Linux a11y route**: [dogtail still ships (2.0 line, Oct 2025)](https://pypi.org/project/dogtail/2.0.dev2/) and does Wayland only via [gnome-ponytail-daemon working around "Wayland does not expose desktop coordinates"](https://modehnal.github.io/) with the RemoteDesktop/ScreenCast APIs — GNOME-shaped, and heavy AT-SPI use [can segfault gnome-shell](http://www.mail-archive.com/desktop-bugs@lists.ubuntu.com/msg833183.html).
- **Where coordinates still beat a11y trees** (the durable niche): [Citrix/VDI/RDP where the app is literally a streamed picture](https://www.accelirate.com/challenges-of-rpa-in-citrix-environment/) — the reason [UiPath built Computer Vision and leads Gartner on Citrix automation](https://medium.com/@praveenb0927/uipath-computer-vision-cv-626387166168); games/canvas/custom-drawn widgets with no a11y layer; [legacy apps without accessibility implementations](https://docs.uipath.com/activities/other/latest/ui-automation/known-issues-and-limitations-citrix); cross-app OS-level flows browsers can't reach; Electron/webview apps where the a11y tree is partial or must be force-enabled. Also note [tauri-driver is still "pre-alpha"](https://v2.tauri.app/develop/tests/) and Playwright's Electron support is experimental — even the modern desktop-app stacks lack solid E2E paths, which is why [TestDriver.ai ($20/mo+, vision-based)](https://testdriver.ai/pricing) and [AskUI ($50–600/mo)](https://checkthat.ai/brands/askui) exist as venture-backed commercial answers.

## 5. Positioning white space — and honest weaknesses

**What would make pixelactions legitimately more than "just use pyautogui":**
1. **Human-verified ground truth + self-healing + asserts is the openQA needle model, portable.** Nobody ships it as a desktop CLI. pyautogui's `locateOnScreen` is the degenerate version (no labels, no provenance, no exit-code contract). The pixelcoords→pixelactions pipeline is "needles for any desktop," reviewable in git.
2. **Wayland is genuinely unserved.** PyAutoGUI never will; pynput can't; xdotool can't; ydotool/dotool are Linux-only primitives with uinput permission friction and no verification. A tool that wraps uinput *and* the libei/portal path cleanly, with documented setup, owns a growing audience (Wayland is now the default on GNOME/KDE distros).
3. **Agent guardrail/tool niche is nascent and sloppy.** MCP desktop servers are pyautogui/robotjs wrappers. A deterministic executor with pre/post point-verification, drift detection (template re-location), and machine-readable failure is a better tool for agents to call — "agent plans, pixelactions replays with receipts."
4. **CI for desktop apps + kiosk/QA rigs**: WinAppDriver's death, tauri-driver's pre-alpha state, and streamed-pixel environments (Citrix, VMs, VNC-only rigs) all reward a coordinate+template executor with exit codes that slots into CI like any other CLI.

**Where the idea is weak — don't kid yourself:**
- **The industry's center of gravity is a11y-first.** Both credible new entrants (terminator, agent-desktop) chose accessibility trees *for* determinism and explicitly avoided coordinates; Power Automate and UiPath treat vision as fallback. Coordinates+templates must be positioned as the layer for where trees don't exist — not as the general answer — or the pitch reads as regression.
- **"Single binary, no setup" is not fully honest on any platform.** Wayland needs uinput udev/capabilities or portal consent prompts; macOS needs Accessibility/Input-Monitoring TCC grants ([once-per-launch prompt quirks included](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/789896)); Windows can't inject into elevated windows from a non-elevated process. Espanso and enigo show it's tractable and show the scar tissue ([Wayland "experimental," layout bugs](https://github.com/espanso/espanso/issues/2162), [libei "currently some bugs"](https://github.com/enigo-rs/enigo)). Budget serious per-platform engineering; enigo is a starting point, not a solution.
- **Coordinate spaces are a swamp** — Windows physical px vs macOS points vs Wayland refusing global coordinates entirely (dotool resorts to percentages; dogtail needed a daemon). Your pixelcoords `CoordSpace` work is the moat here, and it has to be.
- **Solo-maintainer economics in this exact niche are documented misery**: nut.js gave up, TagUI's sponsor walked, xdotool went quiet for four years, SikuliX outlived its maintainer's patience. The counterweight is that computer-use money changed the climate (terminator $2.8M, cua in YC, TestDriver commercial) — but that money is flowing to *agent-native* tools, so the MCP/agent-tool framing is likely the commercially relevant one, with CI/Wayland users as the organic base.

Bottom line: the specific combination — cross-platform incl. Wayland, single binary, declarative files, human-verified coordinate ground truth, template self-healing, assert-gated execution with exit codes — exists nowhere today; its nearest neighbors are Windows-only (terminator), macOS-only (agent-desktop), mobile-only (Maestro), or VM-QA-only (openQA). The gap is real; the risk is not competition but substrate pain (Wayland/macOS/Windows injection) and the a11y-first tide, which the pixelcoords verification story is the right answer to — provided it's positioned as "deterministic execution where trees fail, receipts everywhere."
